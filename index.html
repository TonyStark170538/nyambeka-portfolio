The Story

More than the frame

Nyambeka Bridgit Gichana is a Kenyan model, creative and Bachelor of Science in Applied Computing student based in Nairobi. Her work sits at the intersection of fashion, technology, leadership, sustainability and African identity.

I don't just wear clothes, I tell stories through every frame, every step, every look.

She is building a personal platform that moves from the runway into fashion design, creative direction, digital innovation and impact. Rooted in Kenya and shaped by the world, Nyambeka is developing a future where African creativity is modern, purposeful and globally relevant.

Philosophy
I believe I don’t have to choose one version of myself. I can create, lead, design, learn and model—and each part of me adds to the other. I want to build a career that gives me room to explore all of it.

Nyambeka Bridgit · Miabrita

Purpose

Vision & pillars

I believe every woman has a story worth seeing, and that fashion is one of the many ways we tell it. I aspire to use my presence to celebrate individuality, inspire confidence, and leave an impact beyond the runway.

01

Technology

I’ve always been curious about how things work and how technology can make ideas possible. Studying Applied Computing has given me a space to explore that curiosity, from AI and digital innovation to the ways technology can shape the future of fashion and creativity. I’m interested in where these two worlds meet and what I can create there.

02

Leadership

My leadership journey has taught me that leadership isn’t about having the loudest voice, but about stepping forward and using your voice with purpose. Through my work with AYLF and campus leadership, I’ve learned to take initiative, work with others, and create opportunities that make people feel seen and capable.

03

Sustainability

My interest in sustainability comes from believing that creativity can give things another life. I’m drawn to upcycling and sustainable fashion because they allow me to look at what might be considered waste and imagine what it could become. For me, sustainability is not just about the clothes we make, it’s about being more intentional with what we create, what we consume and the impact we leave behind
